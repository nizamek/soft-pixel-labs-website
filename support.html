CashSlate support

Find an answer below or contact Soft Pixel Labs through the project support page. CashSlate is offline-first, so we cannot access your ledger or recover a forgotten backup password.

Where is my data?

Your ledger is stored on your Android device. CashSlate does not use a sign-in or its own cloud sync. Android system backup and transfer settings may be managed separately in your device settings.

How do I move to a new phone?

Create a password-protected backup in CashSlate, move the file securely, then restore it on the new device. Keep the backup password safe; it cannot be reset by Soft Pixel Labs.

Are exchange rates live?

No. CashSlate uses offline reference values and rates you enter or edit. Check and update the rate when you need a more current conversion.

Can you look at my account?

No. The app has no remote access to your ledger. When reporting an issue, share steps to reproduce it and the app version. Never include account numbers, financial records, phone numbers, or backup files.

Contact Soft Pixel Labs

Report a problem or request an improvement through GitHub Issues. Issues may be public, so do not include personal or financial information.
