Privacy Policy

CashSlate is designed to help you manage personal finances on your Android device. This policy explains what the app stores, what you choose to share, and how you control your information.

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · App: CashSlate for Android · Developer: Soft Pixel Labs

In short: CashSlate has no user account, CashSlate server, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics service. The app has no internet permission. Your entries are stored locally in an encrypted database. Android system backup and transfer settings are controlled by Android and your device manufacturer, as described below.

Information you enter

CashSlate can store financial information you choose to enter, such as account and card names, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, investment values, loan balances, notes, and a person’s name or optional phone number. These details are used on your device to show your balances, summaries, budgets, and history.

CashSlate does not ask you to create an account and Soft Pixel Labs does not receive these entries through the app.

Where your information is stored

The ledger is held in an SQLCipher-encrypted SQLite database in the app’s private Android storage. Its database key is held using Android secure storage. Optional app locking can use device authentication or an app passcode.

CashSlate does not operate a service that uploads or synchronizes your ledger. Android’s system backup and device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on your Android version, manufacturer, and device settings. These operating-system features are separate from CashSlate’s own sync service. Review your device’s backup settings if you want to control system-managed copies.

Backups and exports you choose

You can create a password-protected CashSlate backup file and export transaction data. These actions create files that you control. If you choose a system share sheet, email, file provider, messaging app, or another destination, that destination receives the file according to your choice and its own privacy practices. Keep your backup password separate; Soft Pixel Labs cannot recover it.

People and messaging

Names and optional phone numbers for people you track are stored locally. If you tap the WhatsApp action, CashSlate can open a message draft in WhatsApp using the details you selected. The message is not sent by CashSlate; you review and send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply once you use that service.

Notifications and currency information

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification access is requested only if you enable reminders. CashSlate includes offline reference exchange-rate data and lets you enter or edit rates. It does not fetch live exchange rates or market prices.

Information sharing and tracking

CashSlate does not include ads, analytics, or a CashSlate account service. The app does not transmit your ledger to Soft Pixel Labs. Information leaves the app only when you choose an export, backup destination, or external app such as WhatsApp, or through Android system backup or transfer features described above.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app’s local database until you delete them, use the erase-all-data option, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. A backup or export file you created is separate and must be deleted from its saved location. Copies made by Android system backup or a destination app are subject to that service’s controls and retention rules.

Children

CashSlate is a general personal-finance tool and is not designed for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children through an online service.

Changes to this policy

If CashSlate’s data practices change, Soft Pixel Labs will update this page and revise the effective date. The current policy will remain available at this public URL.

About this website

This policy describes the CashSlate Android app. The Soft Pixel Labs website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process basic technical information when a page is requested. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its website-hosting practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Soft Pixel Labs support page on GitHub. Please do not post financial details, account information, phone numbers, or backup files in a public issue.
